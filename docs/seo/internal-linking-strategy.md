# Internal linking

Researched and implemented on 28 November 2026.

Home and footer lead to the catalogue, project applications, guides and contact. The category landing pages are linked from the products and infrastructure indexes. Model pages link back to their family through product navigation and breadcrumbs, and link onward to applications and guides. Applications link to relevant families or visible architectural references and carry the application into the quote form. Guides link to relevant fixtures, planning topics and technical sources.

Visual navigation continues to support the existing filter queries and catalogue sliders. Canonical breadcrumbs use the permanent category URLs. Use these content relationships to keep new pages connected to actual buyer journeys.

- [Google guidance on ecommerce URL structure](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/designing-a-url-structure-for-ecommerce)
