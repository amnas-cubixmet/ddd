# Content roadmap

Researched and implemented on 28 November 2026.

1. Release the verified catalogue at the primary website origin. Review sitemap discovery, key canonical URLs and product enquiries after release.
2. Obtain model-specific datasheets and image clearance. Add exact optical, electrical and mechanical attributes only after reviewing the material with the owner.
3. Prioritise colour temperature and CRI, beam angles, warehouse layout and controls, high bay versus low bay, outdoor and architectural selection, maintenance and COB versus SMD. Publish when a full useful answer and source evidence exist.
4. Evaluate high mast and stadium landings only after confirming current product support. Add case studies from documented jobs. Evaluate location pages only after confirming location and service coverage.
