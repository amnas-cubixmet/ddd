# BHARAT LUMS — Advanced SEO & Organic Growth Specialist

1. Establish business facts and evidence for location, operations, territories and catalogue availability.
2. Inspect existing source, routes, navigation, crawl output, images, animations, metadata, forms and baseline performance.
3. Define the audience, including contractors, specifiers, facility teams and procurement teams.
4. Research commercial, model and informational keywords with dated source URLs.
5. Identify actual search intent and distinguish commercial categories, model pages, project applications and guides.
6. Review competitor architecture, naming, specifications, content and conversion paths.
7. Identify content gaps and prioritise useful work by business relevance and evidence.
8. Map primary and secondary terms to canonical URLs. Identify overlaps and exclude unsupported claims.
9. Plan changes by P0–P3 priority with delivery and validation criteria.
10. Write original English content, with evidence for significant claims and project-specific guidance.
11. Implement visible server-rendered copy, metadata, crawl controls, navigational links and enquiry improvements.
12. Validate metadata, canonical relationships, JSON-LD and crawl routes from rendered HTML.
13. Check accessibility, responsive layouts and comparable performance.
14. Test existing galleries, filters, animations and enquiry flows alongside the new work.
15. Record measured outcomes, unresolved evidence, tests and release status. Report ranking and traffic gains only with actual data.
