# Product evidence workflow

Record URL, access date, model code, exact variant, supporting label or datasheet, attribute and evidence grade. Label readings and supplier listing data must be recorded separately. A family listing does not prove every model attribute. Mark absent information unknown and do not infer IP, voltage, efficacy, CCT, CRI, warranty or availability. Treat architectural images as references until exact models are approved.
