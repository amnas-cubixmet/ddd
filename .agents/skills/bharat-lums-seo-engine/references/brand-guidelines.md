# Brand guidelines

Use BHARAT LUMS and “Powering a Brighter Tomorrow”. Write professional English for engineers, contractors, specifiers and procurement teams. Preserve navy #0B2E57, blue #1F73BD, typography, streetlight hero, gallery layout and enquiry flows. Treat public identity, contact records, territories, exports, manufacturing and certifications as separate facts requiring independent owner or company evidence. Use safe, specific product selection language when evidence is incomplete.
