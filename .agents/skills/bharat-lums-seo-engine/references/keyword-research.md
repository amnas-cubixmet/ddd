# Keyword research

Search several representative terms in each cluster and record market, source URL, date, result type and dominant intent. Maintain a CSV with keyword, cluster, intent, audience, market, route, primary/secondary, volume, difficulty, relevance, conversion potential, source, research date and implementation status. Mark unavailable measurements unverified. Assign one primary intent per page. Separate catalogue, application, model and learning searches. Treat geographic and manufacturer queries as research only until verified.
